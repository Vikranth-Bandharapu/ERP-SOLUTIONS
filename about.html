Our Story

Building the operational backbone of modern enterprises.

Founded in 2016, Stackly was born out of frustration with legacy ERP systems that were clunky, hard to use, and expensive to maintain. We set out to create a unified platform that combines enterprise-grade power with consumer-grade design.

Today, Stackly powers operations for thousands of organizations worldwide, helping them break down data silos, automate workflows, and make intelligent decisions in real-time.
