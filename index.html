Frequently Asked Questions

How long does implementation take?

Implementation time varies depending on the complexity of your business and data migration needs. However, our average deployment for the Professional plan takes between 3 to 6 weeks.

Can I migrate data from my legacy system?

Yes, absolutely. Stackly ERP provides comprehensive data migration tools and dedicated support engineers to help you securely import data from Excel, legacy databases, and older ERP systems seamlessly.

Is my data secure on the cloud?

Security is our top priority. We use AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Our infrastructure is hosted on AWS with strict SOC 2 compliance, ensuring enterprise-grade protection for all your sensitive information.
